## 4. Prerequisites

No research mathematics, statistics, or machine learning background is required to start:

- **Required**: JavaScript (or Python), basic Node.js, CRUD and APIs, basic Git.
- **Helpful**: React, Express, MongoDB.
- **Not Needed**: Advanced mathematics, statistics, linear algebra, machine learning, or deep learning research. Solid engineering fundamentals are all you need.

<img src="../assets/season-01-inside-the-mind-of-ai/01-welcome-to-namaste-ai/04-prerequisites.jpg" alt="Prerequisites" width="600" />

---

## 5. How to Get the Most Out of This Course

Success requires active engagement rather than passive watching:

- **MYON (Make Your Own Notes)**: Write your own notes in your own words to solidify understanding and retention.
- **Assignments**: Complete every assignment independently without shortcuts.
- **Community**: Participate in discussions and collaborate with peers.
- **Social Media**: Share daily learnings and milestones in public for accountability.
- **Time**: Dedicate focused, uninterrupted hours to practice and reflect.

<img src="../assets/season-01-inside-the-mind-of-ai/01-welcome-to-namaste-ai/05-how-to-get-the-most-out-of-this-course.jpg" alt="How to get the most out of this course" width="600" />

---

## 6. How to Learn From This Course

A practical framework for every lesson:

- **Active Practice**: Do not binge watch. Code along, pause videos to test ideas, break things intentionally, and debug until fixed.
- **Independent Problem Solving**: Make your own notes, complete assignments, build original projects, and figure out solutions independently.
- **Community & Accountability**: Share progress publicly, help others in the community, and enjoy the learning process.

<img src="../assets/season-01-inside-the-mind-of-ai/01-welcome-to-namaste-ai/06-how-to-learn-from-this-course.jpg" alt="How to learn from this course" width="600" />
